Layer Farm — Houses

Every house gets its own M (Male) and F (Female) column, so the two never mix. Up to 3 houses fit on the one-page PDF.

Layer Farm — Flock Management

Closing Stock = Opening Stock + New Stock In − Mortalities − Cull. It fills in automatically, and you can still overwrite it.

Layer Farm — Feeds

Feed Closing Stock = Feed Opening Stock − Feed Consumption. Feed Ratio (g/b/d) = Consumption (kg) × 1000 ÷ Closing Stock birds. Both fill in automatically and can be overwritten.

Layer Farm — Environment

Temperature and light are one reading per house, shared by M and F.
