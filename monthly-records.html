Share between devices (cloud sync)

Connect once on each phone or computer. After that, every record you save is also sent to a Google Sheet in your own Drive, and every device shows the same records. It still works with no internet and catches up later.

Status: Not connected on this device.

Set-up steps (first time)
  1. Think of a secret passphrase (any word, no spaces, e.g. cplpoultry2026kk) and type it below. Use the same one on every device.
  2. Press Copy the script.
  3. Open script.google.com in your browser (on a phone, tick Desktop site in the browser menu first), sign in with your Gmail and tap New project. Delete everything in the editor, paste the script, and press the save icon.
  4. Press Deploy → New deployment. Click the gear and choose Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Press Deploy, then Authorize access (choose your account → Advanced → Go to project → Allow). Your records sheet is created by itself in Google Drive, named CPL Poultry Records (cloud sync).
  5. Copy the Web app URL it shows (ends in /exec) and paste it below, then press Connect & sync.
  6. On your other devices: open this page, type the same passphrase and paste the same URL, then Connect & sync.

Anyone who has both the URL and the passphrase could read your records, so keep them private. Your records are also kept in a Google Sheet in your Drive (CPL Poultry Records (cloud sync)) — open it to see every record.

Backup (important)

Your records live only inside this browser on this device. If you clear the browser data or change phones, they are gone. Download a backup file once a month and keep it safe (e.g. email it to yourself).
